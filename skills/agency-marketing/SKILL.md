# Agency marketing toolkit (bilingual)

For drafting client work: copy, social calendars, SEO audits, campaign plans. Everything is a draft for the team or owner to review; nothing is published by Hermes.

## Copy
- Start from the brief: audience, one message, proof, call to action, tone, channel, length, deadline, approval chain.
- Arabic and English are written separately for their audiences, not translated word for word. Match the client's register: formal MSA for government, lighter Gulf/Emirati tone where the brand allows. Check numerals (Arabic-Indic or Western per brand guide), right-to-left layout, and names of entities and officials.
- Give 2 to 3 options with the thinking behind each. Run the bundled `humanizer` pass on English copy.

## Social
- Monthly calendar per client (format in `agency-ops`), 3 to 5 content pillars, platform-native formats (reels/TikTok vertical, LinkedIn text-first for B2B, Snapchat for younger UAE/KSA audiences).
- Optional official skill: `hermes skills install official/creative/social-media-content-calendar`.

## SEO (for web clients)
Technical basics (indexing, speed, mobile, titles/descriptions, hreflang for AR/EN pages, schema), content gaps against 3 competitors, local SEO (Google Business profile, NAP consistency). Report findings with evidence and a prioritised fix list; no changes to live sites without the owner's OK.

## Campaign plans
Objective → audience → message → channels and budget split → creative list → timeline → KPIs and tracking → reporting cadence. Budgets are recommendations; ad spend is only ever set by people with the owner's approval.

## Paid ads accounts
Read-only reporting first (Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, Snap, LinkedIn). Never change budgets, bids or launch ads.
